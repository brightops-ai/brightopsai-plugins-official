# Anti-detection

Wait and scroll policy for Facebook Marketplace browser loops. Randomize every wait within the ranges below. Never navigate in a fixed cadence or other predictable pattern.

## Loading search results

- After the results page loads, scroll 2-3 times with a random 1-3s pause between scrolls.
- Between search terms, wait 30-60s (use this time for market research).
- When filling the location field, type slowly and pick the autocomplete suggestion.

## Visiting listing pages

- Wait 2-5s between listing navigations.
- Every 5 listings, pause an extra 5-15s.
